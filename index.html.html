Cancellation & Refund Policy

We believe in fair and transparent refund policies for our customers.

🕐 Cancellation Timeline

  • Cancellation 48+ hours before departure — 100% refund
  • Cancellation 24–48 hours before departure — 75% refund
  • Cancellation 12–24 hours before departure — 50% refund
  • Cancellation less than 12 hours before departure — No refund
  • No-show without cancellation — No refund

💳 Refund Process

  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days
  • Amount is credited back to the original payment method (UPI / Card / Wallet)
  • Razorpay processing fees (if any) may be deducted
  • Refund confirmation will be sent via SMS/Email

⚠️ Non-Refundable Cases

  • Bookings cancelled after bus departure time
  • Duplicate bookings (only one will be refunded)
  • Partial journey completion

📞 How to Cancel

To cancel your booking, contact us at support@shahajitravels.in or call our helpline with your booking reference number. Cancellations can also be initiated through the Shahaji Travels mobile app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

📋 Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, and email address during registration
  • Booking details (bus, route, seat, date, passenger info)
  • Payment information (processed securely via Razorpay — we do not store card details)
  • Device information and app usage data for improving our services

🔐 How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your bus bookings
  • To send booking confirmations, e-tickets, and OTPs via SMS
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our platform and services
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

🤝 Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only with our trusted payment gateway partner (Razorpay) and bus operators, solely for the purpose of completing your booking. All third-party partners are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

🛡️ Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, OTP-based authentication, and secure payment processing via PCI-DSS compliant gateways. Your personal data is stored securely on encrypted servers.

👤 Your Rights

  • You may request access to your personal data at any time
  • You may request deletion of your account and data
  • You may opt out of promotional communications
  • For any privacy concerns, contact: support@shahajitravels.in

🍪 Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience. You may disable cookies in your browser settings. Essential cookies required for booking functionality cannot be disabled.
