Privacy Policy

📋 Introduction

Shahaji Travels ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Shahaji Travels mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app to book bus tickets.

By using our app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

📱 Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use our app:

  • Personal Information: Full name, mobile number, email address
  • Passenger Details: Name, age, gender of passengers for booking
  • Booking Information: Journey date, bus route, boarding/dropping points, seat numbers
  • Payment Information: Payment method selected (Cash/UPI/Card). We do not store full card details — payments are processed securely via Razorpay.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, push notification token (for booking alerts)

🎯 How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your bus ticket bookings
  • To send booking confirmation and ticket details
  • To send push notifications about your journey
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our app and services
  • To prevent fraud and ensure secure transactions

🔒 Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All data is transmitted over secure HTTPS connections
  • Payment processing is handled by Razorpay, which is PCI DSS compliant
  • We do not store credit/debit card numbers on our servers
  • User passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text
  • Our backend servers are hosted on secure cloud infrastructure

📤 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in these limited cases:

  • Razorpay: For payment processing only
  • Firebase (Google): For push notifications
  • Bus Operators: Booking details are shared with Shahaji Travels bus operators to fulfill your journey
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or government authority

👶 Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

🔄 Changes to This Policy
